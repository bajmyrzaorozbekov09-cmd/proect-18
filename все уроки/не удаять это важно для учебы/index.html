🏠 Information About the House

This is a modern high-tech style house located on a natural slope surrounded by trees. The architecture combines strict geometric shapes, large glass surfaces, and minimalist design elements

Main features:

Style: High-tech / modern minimalist architecture Shape: Asymmetrical composition with several projecting levels Facade: Light-colored, with smooth concrete or plaster surfaces Windows: Large panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows Balconies: Open terraces with transparent glass railings Lighting: Built-in lights along the paths, stairs, and facade Location: The house is integrated into the natural terrain of a wooded hillside Landscape: Green lawn, wide stairs, stone elements, and pedestrian paths
